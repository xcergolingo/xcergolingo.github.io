CURRENT CHARACTER: MICROSOFT ROCKETBOX FEMALE_ADULT_17

archer-realistic.glb is a conversion of the official Microsoft Rocketbox
Female_Adult_17 model, released by Microsoft under the MIT License.

Official source and original preview:
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/master/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_17
Original FBX:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/master/Assets/Avatars/Adults/Female_Adult_17/Export/Female_Adult_17.fbx
Official license:
https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Conversion modifications: FBX converted to glTF 2.0 GLB using Blender 4.3;
stale source texture paths repaired; original color and normal textures connected
to physically based materials; opaque 2048x2048 color images encoded as JPEG at
quality 92 and normal maps as JPEG at quality 94, both using 4:4:4 chroma;
original 1024x1024 RGBA hair atlas retained losslessly as PNG; hair alpha testing
and two-sided cards enabled; faulty source custom vertex normals replaced with
smooth mesh normals to correct a black shading seam behind the ear;
uniform source scale and skeletal bind pose retained; model translated to center
between feet with soles at ground level and exported Y-up with front toward +Z.
Original face/body geometry, individual bone rest transforms, and skin weights
are not remodeled. The model retains its 80-joint human skeleton and 8,516 source
triangles. Reproduction script: tools/convert-rocketbox-archer.py.

Source example identifies the model as originating from Mixamo:

Adobe's Mixamo FAQ permits royalty-free use of its characters and animations in

https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html

The character is incorporated into this playable game, not offered as a standalone
asset library or stock model download. Model ownership remains with its creators.

The bow, arrows, quiver, continuous archery inverse-kinematic pose, and release
animation were authored specifically for this game. No original Mixamo animation
clips are played by the game. The provided rigged mesh is posed continuously.

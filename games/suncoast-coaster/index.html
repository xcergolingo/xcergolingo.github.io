Learn & ridePass a word or sentence gate to collect it. Listen until the next gate, then practice the next entry. New words arrive each lap.
Choose a rideTry Lagoon Glide, The Sunburst, or Sky Rocket from the park menu or the coaster button at the top. Each has its own route, height, and pace.
Clear your viewTap Hide on the word card, or press V. Pronunciation and collection continue. Tap Show words to bring it back; your choice stays saved.
Your lessonOpen Lesson to choose a language, paste GoLingo JSON or your own lines, and switch between words and sentences. Translation and repeat controls stay saved.
ReviewTap the learning card to hear it again. Open the word basket to replay or export your discoveries for GoLingo.
SoundPronunciation begins when you start. Change speech and repeat in Lesson. The top speaker button controls the coaster’s background sounds separately.
BoostHold the orange button or W / ↑. Recharges when released.
BrakeHold the dark button or Space / ↓ to slow down.
Look aroundDrag the scene. Release to gently face forward.

WELCOME ABOARD

A battle of words.

Find all five enemy ships on a 10×10 grid before your fleet goes down.

  1. 01

    Deploy your fleet

    Select a ship and tap its new starting square, or drag it across your grid. Rotate to change its direction. Leave one square between ships.

  2. 02

    Fire into enemy waters

    Tap a coordinate. An orange burst is a hit; a small blue ring is a miss. Hit every section of a ship to sink it.

  3. 03

    Learn with every hit

    Hits reveal words and play their pronunciation. Sinking a ship unlocks an example sentence when your list includes one. Turn on quizzes in Settings for an extra challenge.

  4. 04

    Choose your battle

    Play against the computer or pass this device between two captains. One Shot alternates turns; Hitting Streak keeps your turn after a hit; Salvo gives each captain three shots.

No timer. Words shuffle with every mission. The enemy never knows your hidden ship positions. In pass-and-play, hand over the device only while the privacy screen is showing.

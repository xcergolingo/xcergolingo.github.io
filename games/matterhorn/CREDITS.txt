REAL ELEVATION DATA
Mapzen / Tilezen Terrarium terrain tiles, using public open elevation sources.
Tile service: https://s3.amazonaws.com/elevation-tiles-prod/terrarium/{z}/{x}/{y}.png
Encoding: height meters = red * 256 + green + blue / 256 - 32768.
Documentation: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/formats.md
Underlying source attribution: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
AWS public dataset: https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
Source region, coordinates and sampled limits: assets/terrain-provenance.json

The foreground piste and chalets are designed and are not an official mapped run.
The referenced screenshot served as art direction; it is not included or used as
an in-game texture. All in-game props, interface art and textures are procedural.

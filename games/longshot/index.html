YOUR FIELD GUIDE

TAKE THE SHOT.

Read the words above each target, then hit a target to hear and collect its word. Pronunciation repeats until you hit another target or pause. Take your time through all three rounds in Relaxed, or enable its optional Timed challenge in Settings.

LISTEN & FIND · SUDDEN DEATH The game randomly speaks one target word. Tap any visible target to hear that target repeatedly, compare what you hear, then use Fire only when you are sure. A correct target gives the next random spoken word. Shooting a different word ends the run immediately and you must start again.

HARD · WALKING PATROLS People walk between cover, change direction and briefly stop. Later rounds get faster. Hard has a round timer and no aim assist. Track each moving word and wait for a clear shot.

ON YOUR PHONE

Drag the open view to aim. Use the left thumbstick to move, Scope to zoom, and the orange Fire button to shoot.

ON A COMPUTER

WASD move · Drag aim · Click / F fire · Space / right-click scope · R reload · Shift steady · H find target · Esc pause.

In Listen & Find, Find becomes Hear so you can replay the spoken prompt, and tapping a target only listens—it does not fire. In other modes, tap Find to face a target. Tap Aa to hide the bottom word card. Settings can hide target words or translations. The sentence button opens an example sentence, and the collection count opens your review list.

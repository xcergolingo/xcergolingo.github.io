Carry words on the wind.

Fly through word gates to listen and collect. Hunt guides your mount to each gate; steer to take the reins. Words keep repeating until the next word or Pause voice.

Any valid language code works: es, ja, zh-CN, ar, and others. JSON can include individual word translations, sentences, and optional audio URLs. Plain sentences are segmented into words; word translations are never guessed.

Your lesson stays in this browser. Audio uses your device’s language voices unless you supply an audio URL. GoLingoApp.com ↗

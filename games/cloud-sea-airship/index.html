Aurelia · Captain's notes

The sky is yours.

Collect the highlighted word beacon above the cloud sea. Use Learn to import a lesson, Replay to hear it, and Hunt to guide the airship. Audio repeats until the next word; the Audio button pauses speech without stopping your flight. Give floating rocks and dark storm clouds a wide berth. There is no timer, and collisions only slow you down.

A FEW LITTLE POINTERS

Find your wings.

On your phone

Hold your phone sideways. Slide the left throttle up for power. Pull the right control down to climb; push it up to descend. Move left or right to bank. Change this in Flight settings → Pitch control.

Tap Acro / Easy beside the throttle while flying. In Acro, hold left or right for a complete roll. With enough speed and height, hold a pitch input for a loop; ease off to stop rotating. In Easy, release the control to help level the wings.

Hold Brake to slow down on the ground. On aircraft with retractable wheels, tap Gear in flight to raise or lower them.

Choose your viewpoint

Choose Camera before you start to set the large view. While flying, tap Camera → Main view for Ground controller, Chase, Cockpit, Nose, either Wing, or Tail. You can switch without pausing.

The Small camera window shows a live view from the plane. Tap its ‹ / › arrows to change position during flight, or choose from its dropdown. Tap × to hide it, and Camera → Small camera to show it again. Changing this window leaves the main view where it is.

Tap Full screen to fill the screen when your browser allows it. An embedded app may require Open game. Info shows or hides instruments and the small word card; words in the flight rings stay visible. Ground controller offers Auto zoom or Wide view in the Camera menu. Tap ← Back at the top left to return directly to aircraft selection.

Take off & fly a jet

Select Runway above Start flight. Add throttle, let the plane build speed, then gently raise the nose. Choose In air for an immediate flying start.

Turbine thrust builds gradually. The Engine readout shows the power delivered. Change throttle early on approach and allow more room for faster passes and turns.

To land, lower the gear, line up with the white centerline, reduce power and descend gently. Hold Brake after touchdown. Crashes are recoverable with a restart.

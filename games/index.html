A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you jump in.

Small questions. A clear place to start.

Do I play here or inside GoLingo?+

Watch the previews and try sample browser demos here. To practice with your own GoLingo word lists, download the app and launch a game from your list.

Can I use my own words and languages?+

Yes. Choose your learning language, your translation language, and the vocabulary you want to practice in GoLingo. The games accept word lists from the app. Their browser demos also include word-import controls.

Are these videos the actual games?+

Yes. The previews are screen recordings of the linked browser games with demonstration vocabulary, not concept animations. They are silent. The layout and performance may vary by device.

How does pronunciation work?+

The browser games use the language voices available on your device. Voice availability and quality depend on the language and device. Turn on sound in a game to hear the words.

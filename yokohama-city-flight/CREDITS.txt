Changes made for this game: selected the Yokohama mobile tiles for the game;
prepared conservative, quarter-metre-quantized collision heightfields from the
provided LOD1 building and terrain meshes; integrated scenery into the flight
simulation with game lighting, water, scenery additions, and flight activities.

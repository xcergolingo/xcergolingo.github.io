Expanded skyline integration
All 23 original Yokohama tiles have a self-hosted distant representation in
skyline/. Most are derived from source LOD3 using gltfpack 1.3 simplification;
two landmark tiles retain original LOD3 geometry, and two boundary-sensitive
tiles use a gentler pass. The original mobile variants remain in models/ for
nearby detail. Both representations use the same tile offsets and preserve
source materials, colors and embedded textures. No extra buildings or geographic
fill were added. Distant details are approximations of the original geometry.

Scenery presets keep all source tiles in view after background loading: low
uses up to four nearby mobile meshes; auto uses up to eight; high uses up to
fourteen, subject to the host's maxTiles cap. Collision heightfields remain
loaded for every tile, independent of the visible mesh detail.

After regenerating the original city with scripts/prepare-city.py, run
scripts/prepare-skyline.py to restore skyline assets and manifest metadata.

The final distant set has 344,628 triangles and occupies 4,457,636 bytes, versus
1,549,742 triangles and 16,163,152 bytes in the original source LOD3 set.
Per-tile geometry extent differences are at most 1.51 m, and maximum roof
heights differ by less than 0.03 m. The two retained landmark tiles were selected
after geometry silhouette checks. Collision grids remain entirely unchanged.

Listen & fly advances your lesson every 14 seconds while you fly. Autopilot is useful for sightseeing and listening; manually fly to the displayed heading in Word gates mode. Use Next to practice without reaching a gate.

For takeoff: release brakes, set full thrust, then pull back gently above 100 knots. For landing: lower gear and flaps, approach runway 36 from the south at about 110–125 knots, and reduce thrust before touchdown.
